## 🎬 **PRESENTATION TIPS**

### **Delivery Style**
- **Confident and enthusiastic** - This is revolutionary
- **Pace yourself** - 2 minutes is tight, practice timing
- **Use pauses** - Let key points sink in
- **Eye contact** - Connect with the audience

### **Visual Cues**
- **Point to screen** - Direct attention to key visuals
- **Use gestures** - Emphasize important points
- **Smile** - Show excitement about the technology
- **Lean forward** - Show engagement and passion

### **Voice Modulation**
- **Start strong** - Hook them immediately
- **Build excitement** - Increase energy as you go
- **Pause for impact** - Let key points resonate
- **End with conviction** - Leave them wanting more

### **Timing Breakdown**
- **0-15s**: Hook and introduction
- **15-45s**: Problem and solution
- **45-75s**: Technical innovation
- **75-105s**: Business impact
- **105-120s**: Competitive advantage and CTA
