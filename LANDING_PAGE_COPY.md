## 🎬 **HOW IT WORKS SECTION**

### **Step-by-Step Process**

#### **Step 1: Customer Initiates Purchase**
*"I want to buy a coffee"*

**What happens:**
- sofIA's Orchestrator Agent understands the intent
- Fetches product catalog and pricing
- Presents options in natural conversation

#### **Step 2: Product Selection**
*"I'll take the cappuccino"*

**What happens:**
- sofIA Payment Agent creates AP2 Intent Mandate
- Displays pricing and payment options
- Maintains conversation context

#### **Step 3: Payment Method Selection**
*"PIX please"*

**What happens:**
- Creates cryptographically signed Cart Mandate
- Generates secure payment link
- Customer completes payment in banking app

#### **Step 4: Payment Confirmation**
*"Payment completed"*

**What happens:**
- BEMOBI Integration Agent verifies payment
- Creates AP2 Payment Mandate
- Sends confirmation and order details
